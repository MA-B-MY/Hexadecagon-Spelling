HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

  • [W] / [↑] : Jump (Press twice to Double Jump!)
  • [A] / [D] : Move Left / Right
  • [SPACE] : Submit your completed word
  • [P] : Pause Game

🎯 The Rules

  • Collect floating letters to build words (min. 2 letters).
  • Submit valid words with the Spacebar to score points!
  • ⚠️ DANGER: If you spell a fake word, you explode!
  • ⚠️ DANGER: Don't let obstacles push you off the left screen!
